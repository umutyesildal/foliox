# FolioX — 60-Second Loom Script

Five slides, twelve seconds each. First person, casual and confident — like explaining it to a friend, not a jury. Each block below has what should be on screen and the exact words to say (~30 words ≈ 12 seconds).

---

## Slide 1 — Hook (~12s · 32 words)

**On screen:** the title card — "Create an index. Own your thesis." — with the SOLANA / LIVE ON DEVNET badges.

**Say:**
> Tokenized stocks are blowing up — you can already buy Nvidia or Apple on-chain. But you can only buy them one at a time. I built something to fix that. It's called FolioX.

## Slide 2 — The gap (~12s · 31 words)

**On screen:** the three big numbers — +422%, $25B, 0.

**Say:**
> This year, tokenized equities grew four hundred twenty-two percent. Kraken's xStocks alone did twenty-five billion in volume. Yet on Solana, there are exactly zero ways to bundle them into a thesis.

## Slide 3 — What FolioX does (~12s · 32 words)

**On screen:** the three cards — Create, Mint, Redeem.

**Say:**
> FolioX makes it three steps. Create a basket with fixed, immutable weights. Mint by depositing the actual stocks — you get one share token. Redeem anytime, and it burns back into the stocks.

## Slide 4 — Why blockchain (~12s · 28 words)

**On screen:** "Only possible here. Only possible onchain." with the two Solana / Onchain cards.

**Say:**
> Why a chain? Because "you can always take your stocks back" has to be code, not a promise. On devnet, redemption worked while one basket stock was paused.

## Slide 5 — The ask (~12s · 33 words)

**On screen:** "Next stop: the global Solana Hackathon." with the three roadmap items and the repo line.

**Say:**
> All of this works today — three programs, three live baskets, and I'm user number one, trading my own baskets on devnet. Next stop: the global Solana Hackathon. Create an index. Own your thesis.

---

**Total: ~156 words ≈ 60 seconds at a relaxed pace.** Practice it once out loud before recording — if you land under 55 seconds, slow down, don't add words.

## Recording tips

- Keep the cursor still — park it in a corner and advance slides with the arrow keys, not clicks.
- Hard-refresh the deck page right before hitting record so fonts and the first slide load clean.
- Never say "ETF" or "fund" — it's a "strategy basket" or "index".
